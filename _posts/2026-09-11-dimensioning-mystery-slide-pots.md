I was recently working on a project involving slide potentiometers and wanted to look for a cheap source to pick up ~10 of them (only needed 5, but wanted to get some extras in case).

![amazon page image](/imgs/slidepots/amz_pg.png)

I broke them out to a breadboard and validated my initial project design and the next step required finding their dimensions for the PCB design. Additionally, I couldn't find any link to a datasheet on Amazon even after reviewing the original page and looking for similar components.

An online search for similar B103 slide pots yielded a near-identical looking component with a wiring + dimensioning diagram at the [following link](https://www.sc-qixing.com/sale-41455993-b103-75mm-mixer-fader-b10k-dual-channel-straight-sliding-potentiometer-integrated-circuits.html).
